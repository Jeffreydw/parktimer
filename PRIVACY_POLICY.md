# Privacy Policy – JW ParkTimer

**Last updated:** 18 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the iPhone app **JW ParkTimer** ("the app") handles your information. The app is made by JW.

## Summary

JW ParkTimer does **not** collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app uses stays on your iPhone.

- No account or sign-up
- No advertising
- No analytics or tracking
- No data sent to us or to third parties

## Information the app uses

### Location (optional)

If you turn on **"Save parking location"**, the app asks for permission to use your location. When you start a parking session, your iPhone's current location is read **once** to remember where you parked.

- The location is stored **only on your device**.
- It is **never sent** to us or to anyone else.
- It is **deleted** when you end your parking session.
- You can refuse or withdraw location access at any time in **Settings ▸ ParkTimer ▸ Location**. The timer and reminders still work without it.

### Notifications

The app schedules reminders before and when your parking time expires. These notifications are created and delivered **locally on your iPhone**. No data is sent to a server. You can turn notifications off at any time in **Settings ▸ ParkTimer ▸ Notifications**.

### Live Activity

The countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is generated on your device. No data is sent to a server.

### Settings and parking session

Your current parking session (start time, end time and, if enabled, the parked location) and your chosen reminder time are stored on your device so the app can continue after it is closed. Like other app data, this may be included in your device backup (for example iCloud Backup), which is managed by you and Apple.

## Apple services

- **Directions:** When you tap **"Walk to My Car"**, the parked location is passed to the Apple Maps app to show directions. From that moment, Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple Maps.
- **Purchases:** The app is sold through the App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Because we do not collect or store any personal data, we hold no data about you that could be viewed, corrected or deleted. All data used by the app is on your own device and is removed when you delete the app.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we do not share them with anyone.
